Teacher Guidance:
where the work stands

A framework that records a large teacher privately grading every step a small student agent takes, revealing only a code-enforced dose of feedback — then distils those traces into a student that needs no teacher at all. The machine is built and proven on HotpotQA. The corpus it was built to produce has not been generated yet.

01

The three-sentence verdict

The framework is production-grade

471 tests, resumable multi-GPU runners, a three-tier teacher cost router with a circuit breaker, leakage enforced in code, per-call cost and provenance telemetry, a trajectory explorer, and converters for four multi-hop datasets.

The science already pays off

Guidance-SFT lifts Qwen3.5-0.8B from 3.0% to 60.2% judge-correct with no teacher at inference — beating the untrained student with a live 120B teacher in the loop, at 57% fewer tokens.

The pivot stalled mid-flight

The multi-dataset corpus is designed, its converters written and tested — but no tg_v1 dataset exists, no collection plan has been generated, no non-HotpotQA episode has ever been produced, and the machine that ran all of this is gone.

02

What the system does

Per question: the student optionally drafts a plan the teacher critiques, then emits one JSON tool action per step, up to a step budget. The environment executes the tool deterministically against that question's own local paragraph set — no web, fully reproducible. The teacher, who can see the gold answer, grades the step privately. A renderer then exposes only what the guidance level permits, and a leakage checker sanitizes it before the student ever reads it.

Nine tools: search, extract, verify, decompose, reformulate, synthesize, finish, wiki_read, wiki_write. The split between the teacher's private diagnosis and the student's visible guidance is the corpus's central asset — and it is enforced by code, not by prompting, which is exactly what a reviewer will probe.

The supervision ladder

skip
Control arm. No teacher call at all.
G0
Binary reward. A 0 or a 1.
G1
Continuous reward. A score from 0.0 to 1.0.
G2
Outcome feedback. Score plus a short note.
G3
Diagnostic feedback. Score plus a diagnosis, no next action. The workhorse setting.
G4
Scaffolded guidance. Score, diagnosis, and a next-step hint.

Holding question, student and seed fixed while moving only this dial is what turns a trace dump into a counterfactual corpus — and what supports paired, per-question significance tests instead of cross-population comparisons.

03

What the experiments showed

Distillation — the headline result

Four arms on 100 fresh unseen questions, five seeds, budget 4, vLLM, judged by an independent gpt-oss-120b. The m1 arm is the student trained so the teacher's guidance became its own internal thought, running with no teacher at inference.

For both Qwen students the internalized model beats the untrained model with a live 120-billion-parameter teacher in the loop, while dropping that teacher entirely. For granite — much the strongest base model — internalization keeps 76% of the teacher-in-loop accuracy. Format adherence is a training outcome too: invalid actions fell from 51 to 2 on dev, and the base model ends 75% of episodes without ever committing an answer where m1 commits in ~99%.

Cross-student transfer — trace quality beats trace ownership

Qwen3.5-0.8B trained twice over the same 3,000 questions: once on its own trajectories, once on granite's. Semantic accuracy is a dead heat (52.8% vs 53.7%, inside seed noise) — but the granite-trained model has 3.6× the exact-match, +14 F1, finishes voluntarily 2.4× as often, produced zero invalid actions across 3,600 episodes, and evaluates 22% faster. Answer form and efficiency habits transfer from whoever authored the traces.

265 of the 3,000 questions were never solved by any run; an escalating retry campaign at budgets 4 → 20 → 30 later recovered 118 of them, and the remainder became the golden-100 hardest-question test set. Hiding the budget from the student measurably improves both correctness and efficiency — the student stops treating the budget as a quota. And in the 4-student × 5-setting protocol matrix, the hidden max-20 setting won for every model, while the no-teacher arm lost for every model.

What guidance costs

Over one 3,000-episode run: 85.8M input and 13.7M visible output tokens. The teacher accounts for 57.6% of all billed tokens, and its reasoning overhead — billed output versus visible output — is 2.82×. That ratio is the single biggest cost lever in the whole corpus plan, and the reason the internalized student's token economics matter as much as its accuracy.

Read the metrics as a set

Cover-match over-credits verbose answers, exact-match and F1 under-credit them, and the teacher verdict (≥ 0.40) is lenient. They disagree materially — in the wiki A/B, one arm's teacher-rate was flat while its cover-match halved. Every table above should be read alongside the others, never averaged.

04

How a new dataset becomes traces

The engine is already generic. A new multi-hop dataset needs no engine change — only two JSONL shapes: questions carrying gold answers, supporting titles and a per-question candidate document set, and a corpus of those candidate paragraphs. Four converters exist; each one's output is validated row by row.

01
prepare_dataset.py --dataset {hotpotqa|2wikimultihopqa|musique|strategyqa}Converts a source into canonical questions + corpus + a manifest recording conversion stats, license, SHA-256 of both files and the framework commit. Gold granularity is represented, not faked: paragraph-level sources report a null supporting-fact recall rather than a zero that would silently drag every cross-dataset average down.
02
smoke_datasets.py --data-root …Offline preflight, free and in seconds: schema conformance, unique qids, every candidate document present — and the check that actually matters, that the real retriever can reach each question's gold documents. A converter can emit valid rows the retriever cannot serve.
03
probe_models.pyIs each teacher source alive, and does its verdict JSON parse? Reasoning teachers hide their chain-of-thought in three different places; a naive parser returns the model's discarded draft instead of its verdict.
04
plan_collection.pyPartitions rather than crosses: each of the 12 teacher×student combinations gets its own question batch plus a shared 500-question anchor set. Anchors make teachers and students comparable on identical items; the disjoint remainder buys breadth at bounded cost. Never run.
05
smoke_matrix.py --plan …A handful of real episodes per matrix cell, audited end to end, before six figures of episodes are spent. Never run against the current teacher roster.
06
run_tg_vllm.py / run_batched_parallel.pyMass generation, resumable — re-running a slice continues where it stopped. Then validate every run for leakage and integrity.
07
build_release.pyThe boundary between raw and published: drops per-call cost, provider response bodies and the router chain, and emits a full view with the teacher's privileged reasoning and a train_safe view with it stripped — because fine-tuning naively on the full view trains a model on text containing the gold answer.

Open bugs worth fixing before spending GPU-hours

  • Voluntary "unknown" finishes the teacher accepts. Episodes stop after 2–7 steps, well under budget, on a voluntary finish of "unknown" that the teacher then accepts. Two candidate fixes were designed; neither is implemented. This silently costs correctness in every run.
  • The wiki A/B analyzer reports two metrics as permanently zero — it matches the wrong edit-op vocabulary, so anyone reading its stats file programmatically gets silent zeros.
  • Teacher capacity is unresolved. At the last probe, one teacher was free and working, one was billed, one returned a deterministic gateway error, and every fallback key was out of credit. That is the single largest risk to a six-figure-episode run.

07

Decisions only you can make

Each of these blocks scheduling, not engineering. They are cheap to answer and expensive to leave open.

  1. Target venue and deadline. Everything downstream is sized by this one date.
  2. Scale. The full ~184K-episode design, or the trimmed variant? The anchor-set design keeps the paired comparisons valid either way.
  3. Dataset set. Confirm all four sources, or drop the riskiest — the boolean one that has never been converted.
  4. Teacher roster. Depends on the re-probe and the license audit. If only one teacher is both free and working, the teacher-scaling study needs paid budget or a local small teacher.
  5. Budget cap. Free-gateway-first keeps cost near zero; a fully paid run is roughly $550, realistically $150–400.
  6. Human annotation. Who annotates, with what budget and what ethics approval?
  7. The existing pilot traces. Regenerate them under v1 configs, or ship them as a documented v0?
