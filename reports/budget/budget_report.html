Basis of estimate

Every figure comes from episodes actually run and actually billed — EdenAI returns a real USD cost per call, which is summed across every logged LLM call in an episode. Nothing here is a list-price extrapolation.

Configurationg3_plan — guidance level 3, plan review (3 rounds), step budget 3, budget hidden
Studentfau/ibm-granite/granite-4.1-3b — free FAU gateway, contributes $0
Teacher endpointhttps://api.edenai.run/v3/chat/completions
Sample5 episodes per teacher (HotpotQA) · 3 episodes per dataset (Qwen3.8)

A teacher-guidance episode costs $0.042–$0.095. Per-episode spend is set by the protocol — roughly four teacher calls per episode — far more than by question difficulty. The teacher is effectively 100% of the bill; the student is free.

06

Cost levers

  • Run bulk generation on the free FAU gateway. DeepSeek-V4-Flash and gpt-oss-120b were both verified working and free on 2026-08-30. Paid teachers are only needed where teacher identity is the variable under study. This is the difference between €865 and near-zero for the bulk corpus.
  • Fix GLM-5.3 before buying any of it. It needs an 8,000-token cap and an EdenAI timeout ≥ 600 s. It expands its reasoning to fill whatever cap it is given (16k → 5,943 tokens, $0.0243), so 8,000 is a sweet spot, not a floor.
  • Step budget drives cost roughly linearly. These figures are budget 3; plan a budget-5 run on the deeper datasets at ~$0.07–0.08 per episode.
  • Wall-clock may bind before money does. At ~200 s of teacher API time per episode, 12,000 episodes is ~660 hours serialized. EdenAI concurrency limits are unmeasured.
07

Caveats

  • n is small — five episodes per teacher, three per dataset. The 30% contingency exists for this reason.
  • Nine of twelve matrix cells are derived, not measured.
  • GLM-5.3 is an upper bound, pending a corrected run.
  • Student deviation: granite-4.1-3b on FAU, not local granite-4.2 — every GPU node was draining for a reboot, and 4.2 is not in the FAU catalogue. Student calls are free either way, so this affects trajectory shape, not cost.
  • Accuracy is not a cost figure but is reported because it decides how many episodes are needed: 4/5 correct on HotpotQA against 1/3 on 2Wiki and MuSiQue.
