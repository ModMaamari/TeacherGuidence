m1-LODO · leave-one-dataset-out study

A 3B student that never saw the dataset

A teacher critiques a small model at every step of a multi-hop retrieval task. Train the model so it writes that critique itself, and it keeps most of the benefit with no teacher at inference — including on a dataset it was never trained on, where it reaches 80–87% of the far larger teacher's score.

01

What was tested

During data collection a large teacher watched a small student solve multi-hop questions and critiqued every step. The student never saw the gold answer; the teacher's feedback was rendered through a leakage gate.

m1 turns that critique into the student's own first thought. Each training target begins with a teacher_guidance block — the critique the student used to receive — followed by its thought and action. At inference there is no teacher: the model writes its own guidance, then acts on it.

The question this study answers is whether that skill is general or merely dataset-specific. Four models were trained, each held one dataset out entirely, and each was tested on the dataset it had never seen.

02

Datasets and splits

Four multi-hop QA datasets, 2,000 questions each (StrategyQA 1,999). Every question id was hashed once into two pools, so a question can never leak between training and test through a second episode.

Held-in questions belong to a dataset the model trained on, but were themselves withheld. Unseen = the entire dataset, from a model that never trained on it.

04

How every model was evaluated

Not next-token prediction. Every test question is solved by the model itself, in the same agent loop the corpus was collected with:

01
Plan. The model writes a plan for the question.
02
Act, three times. One JSON action per step — search, extract, verify, decompose, reformulate, synthesize, finish — executed deterministically against that question's own candidate paragraphs. No web, gold never shown.
03
Commit an answer, voluntarily or forced at the budget.

Budget 3, plan review on, budget hidden, greedy decoding, fixed seed, vLLM backend — identical for base, students and teacher. No teacher guides anything at evaluation time, including the teacher arm, which solves the questions alone.

Metrics

  • judge-correct — an independent LLM (Kimi-K2.6, falling back to MiniMax-M3 then Mistral-Medium) sees only question, gold and prediction. It is the headline: string metrics understate every arm.
  • cover-match — deterministic: gold contained in the prediction, or ≥60% of gold's content tokens present, with guards against partial-entity matches.
  • exact match / F1 — normalized string equality and token overlap.
  • doc recall — supporting-document retrieval; separates "never found the evidence" from "found it and failed to compose".
Why the judge matters

Cover-match understates the teacher by 4 to 13 points, and unevenly: +0.6 on 2Wiki, +13.2 on StrategyQA, where boolean answers arrive as prose ("Yes, the Japanese spider crab can…") and exact match collapses to 0.011. Ranking datasets on string metrics alone would have been misleading.

The finding

Training on a dataset buys only 4–10 points over never having seen it. Every never-trained score sits far closer to the trained-on score than to base. On HotpotQA the model that never trained on it scored higher than the models that did. The skill being learned is not "how to answer 2Wiki questions" — it is how to guide oneself through a multi-hop retrieval task, and that transfers.

08

What else changed with training

It learns when to stop

Voluntary finishing rises from 0–8% (base) to 4–19% (trained). The base model almost always burns the full budget; the trained model commits an answer once it has one.

It emits valid actions

Invalid-action rates fall on MuSiQue from 2.4% to 0.6% and on StrategyQA from 2.7% to 1.3%. Format adherence is a training outcome, not a side effect.

It costs almost nothing extra

Tokens per episode rise only 4–7% (4,884→5,263 on MuSiQue), despite the model now generating a guidance block before every action.

Retrieval barely moves

Doc recall changes little (0.581→0.665 on MuSiQue, 0.789→0.812 on HotpotQA). The gain is in composition, not in finding documents.

09

Reading the datasets against each other

MuSiQue is hardest for everyone — base 0.138, student 0.368, teacher 0.533 — and it is the dataset explicitly built to resist shortcut answering. Its doc recall is also lowest (0.581–0.665), so part of the difficulty is retrieval, but the teacher retrieves at 0.770 and still only reaches 0.533: the wall is multi-hop composition.

StrategyQA shows the largest training effect (+48 points, base 0.189 → 0.669) and is the one dataset where the student matches the teacher. Its questions need implicit decomposition rather than span extraction, which the base model simply cannot do and the guidance format teaches directly.

2Wiki is easiest for trained models (0.738–0.792) yet its exact match stays low (0.270) — the answers are right but verbose. That divergence is why judge-correct leads this report.

10

Guarantees and limitations

What is guaranteed

  • No test question was trained on. Splits are by sha256(qid); an independent audit re-asserted disjointness on the built files — zero overlap in all four folds.
  • No gold leaked into training. The same audit found no target stating a gold answer the prompt never showed, no teacher_private_diagnosis text in any target, and no malformed target.
  • ~90 examples per fold were dropped because they came from episodes that were correct but ungrounded — a forced finish committing the right answer while the teacher's own feedback said the evidence was missing. Training on those teaches confident hallucination.
  • The judge is independent of the model that produced the training data.

What limits these results

  • One student model and one teacher family. This shows that this guidance transfers, not that the effect is universal.
  • The teacher arm is a sample (~250 questions per unseen set, 1,755 total), so its figures carry wider error bars than the students'. Section 7 is the paired comparison that controls for this.
  • Budget 3 binds. Roughly 93% of collection episodes hit the step limit; a larger budget could change the ranking.
  • Contamination. HotpotQA and 2Wiki are almost certainly in the base model's pretraining. MuSiQue is the most trustworthy transfer signal — and the one with the widest student–teacher gap.
  • Held-in sets are small (170–203 questions) against 2,000 for unseen, so trained-on figures are noisier than never-trained ones.
  • Two of 18,986 student episodes received no usable judge verdict and are excluded from judge columns.
